How We Work

How We'll Work Together

Simple, four steps, no surprises along the way.

01

Brief & Discovery

You share your business, your audience, and what you want the logo to say. I ask questions until the brief is clear enough to actually design from.

Deliverables
  • Project brief
  • Reference moodboard
  • Timeline & scope confirmed
02

Concepts & Sketches

I develop 2–3 distinct logo directions based on the brief — each with its own reasoning, not just random variations of the same idea.

Deliverables
  • 2–3 logo concepts
  • Rationale for each direction
  • First revision round
03

Refinement

We narrow down to one direction and refine it — spacing, weight, color, and how it holds up at small sizes and on different backgrounds.

Deliverables
  • Refined final logo
  • Color & black/white versions
  • Application preview mockups
04

Delivery & Handoff

Once approved, you get every file format you'll actually need — organized and ready to use, with no guessing which file goes where.

Deliverables
  • AI, EPS, PNG & PDF files
  • Basic usage guide
  • Source files
Good Fit?

Is This A Good Fit?

This is a fit if:

  • You need a professional logo or identity, not just a quick sketch
  • You're a business ready to invest in how you look, wherever you're based
  • You want a designer who asks questions instead of guessing
  • You're comfortable with an advance-payment, project-based process

This is not a fit if:

  • You're looking for a $5 logo with unlimited free changes
  • You need a full agency team rather than one dedicated designer
  • You're not ready to pay an advance before work begins
